I grew up watching infrastructure fail. Power cuts. Fiber cuts. M-Pesa timeouts during peak traffic. I learned that reliability is not a feature. It is the foundation.

Today I run three experiments in African infrastructure from Karen, Nairobi:

africalatency.dev — Fixing the digital layer

DNS, CDN, BGP, and TLS optimization for African fintech APIs.

Brilliant Unicorn — Fixing the physical layer

Solar connections and energy solutions for Kenyan businesses.

Kudu Stack — Fixing the ecological layer

Edge-AI wildlife telemetry, water prediction, and carbon compliance.

I code in Rust, Go, and Python. I hold an MSc in Cyber Security from OUK. I understand Safaricom peering, KPLC outages, and conservancy ranger workflows because I live with them. You cannot build this from San Francisco.

"I design systems that survive audits, scale, and time."
