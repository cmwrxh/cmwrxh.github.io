About AfricaLatency

We help businesses build better digital experiences.

AfricaLatency helps businesses understand why their websites and digital services can feel slow — especially for people using them across Africa. We find the problems, explain them clearly, and help businesses fix them.

Run a free scan →Start a conversation
Why we exist

AfricaLatency is a specialist company focused on one specific problem: making sure digital products actually work well for people across Africa. A lot of apps and websites are built and tested in Europe or the US, then launched everywhere — including Africa — without anyone checking whether they actually perform well here. We fix that gap.
