The problem

Africa is not a single performance environment.

A service can look healthy from a global synthetic monitor while users on a particular African ISP, route, mobile network or cloud path experience something very different.

01

Global averages hide local failures.

Country, network, routing, edge placement and infrastructure choices all change the path between a user and an application. AfricaLatency makes those differences measurable.

02

Performance needs an engineering answer.

We do not stop at a slow number. The objective is to identify the layer responsible, establish evidence, and give the engineering team a practical sequence of fixes.
