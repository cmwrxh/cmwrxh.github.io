Acceptable Use Policy

Our services must be used responsibly, lawfully and with proper permission.

Allowed use

Use AfricaLatency services only for lawful business, research, monitoring and performance work that you are authorized to conduct.

You must not

You must not use our services to attack, disrupt, damage, overload, scan or gain unauthorized access to systems, networks or accounts. You must not use our services for fraud, harassment, malware distribution, credential theft, unlawful surveillance or any other illegal activity.

Authorized testing

Only test systems and services where you have permission to do so. If an engagement involves a third-party system, you are responsible for ensuring that the required authorization has been obtained.

Our response

We may pause or stop a service where we reasonably believe it is being misused, creates a security risk, violates this policy, or may expose another party to harm.

Questions

If you are unsure whether a planned activity is allowed, contact us before starting it.
