Infrastructure context

Exchange points and international connectivity matter.

KIXP in Nairobi

The Kenya Internet Exchange Point (KIXP) operates in Nairobi and provides an environment for networks to exchange traffic locally through peering. Its current infrastructure includes multiple Nairobi facilities and a Mombasa location.

Source: KIXP and Internet Society Pulse, September 2026.

Submarine connectivity

Kenya's international connectivity includes submarine cable landing infrastructure at Mombasa. Communications Authority material identifies systems including EASSy, TEAMS and SEACOM as landing there, while later regulatory material continues to treat submarine cable landing as a distinct part of Kenya's international connectivity infrastructure.

Source: Communications Authority of Kenya.
