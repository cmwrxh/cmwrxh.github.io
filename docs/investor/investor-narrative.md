# AfricaLatency: investor narrative
AfricaLatency is building performance intelligence for websites and APIs serving African markets. The product direction combines Lighthouse audits with latency measurements requested from public community probes in nine African countries: Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Egypt, Ghana, Morocco, Tanzania, Uganda and Senegal. Probe availability varies; these probes are not owned by AfricaLatency.

Scanner and report code exist in the repository, but the production deployed commit and end-to-end production evidence still need to be attached. Real-user metrics (RUM) are beta. Customer monitoring is beta/in progress. Alerts, public status pages and AI/MCP are not presented as production-ready.

AfricaLatency is pre-seed. The founder reports no revenue yet. This pack makes no claim of paid customers, recurring revenue, retention, testimonials, uptime, SLA, validated speed improvement or gross margin. “74% speed improvement” and “44/44 Safaricom/Airtel” are excluded because source data, methodology, dates, sample sizes and permission have not been verified.

Proposed pricing includes a KES 3,500 audit, USD $19 Speed Check and Custom Deep Dive from KES 25,000. Monitoring/RUM pricing remains to be confirmed. Proposed prices are not sales or margin evidence. Fundraising target and use of funds are open questions.

Next milestones: (1) dated reproducible scan-to-report demo with deployed SHA and CI; (2) verify tenant isolation, SSRF controls, payment webhook behavior, backup/recovery and security-advisor results; (3) test RUM/monitoring in preview with clearly named test entities and scheduler/alerts off; (4) collect approved pilot evidence and first paid invoices; (5) calculate unit costs from actual invoices and usage exports.
