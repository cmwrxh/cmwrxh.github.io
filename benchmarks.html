Measurement rules

Methodology and comparability

Point-in-time scans

Measurements describe the public endpoint and test profile at the time of a scan. They can change with network conditions, content, device profile and origin load.

Regional averages

Regional TTFB is published only when the underlying probe results are available and the aggregation method is documented. Missing probe results remain unavailable, not zero.

AI crawler access

Robots.txt permission and observed crawler requests are different signals. Permission does not prove a crawler visited the site; live activity requires verified origin-log data.
