Methodology

These are point-in-time public endpoint scans. Nairobi Mobile LCP is a Lighthouse mobile lab measurement, not field data. Regional Avg TTFB is calculated only from valid regional probe measurements returned by the scan. AI crawler access reflects robots.txt permissions, not proof of actual crawler visits. Missing probe results are excluded, never estimated.
