The problem

Latency numbers alone don't tell you what to fix.

Users in some African markets experience slow applications or APIs. You don't know whether the cause is network routing, cloud region, CDN, backend processing, database performance, or third-party services. Your engineering team needs evidence and a prioritized remediation plan — not just a latency number.

What's included

A measured diagnosis from baseline to remediation.

  • Performance baseline across selected African locations
  • Country/network comparison
  • Bottleneck and root-cause analysis
  • Infrastructure architecture review
  • Prioritized remediation roadmap
  • Expected impact of proposed fixes, clearly labeled as estimates
  • Optional post-remediation validation
