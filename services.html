How an engagement works

A simple path from problem to proof.

01

Inquiry

Tell us the product, markets, endpoints and performance concern.

02

Qualification

We confirm scope, measurement targets, access needs and authorization boundaries.

03

Scope & payment

We agree the package, deliverables, timeline and pilot price before testing begins.

04

Measure

We run authorized diagnostics across the agreed African markets and networks.

05

Diagnose

We separate network, routing, edge, server, application, database and dependency effects.

06

Report & validate

You receive evidence-linked findings, a prioritized roadmap and an optional validation plan.

Engagement principle

Measure. Diagnose. Fix. Validate.

We start with evidence. The output is not a generic checklist; it is a prioritized technical roadmap explaining what is slow, why it is slow, what should be fixed first, and how to prove the fix worked.
