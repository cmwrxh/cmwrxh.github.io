Built for regional context

Your users are local. Your performance testing should be, too.

A page that feels fast from one region can feel very different across another route, mobile carrier or access network. AfricaLatency helps teams ask better questions about performance across African networks using measurements, not assumptions.

Why regional context matters

Global averages can hide the conditions experienced by a meaningful share of your users.

Routes are not uniform

Latency depends on the path between the user, network, and service. Geography alone does not tell the whole story.

Mobile is a first-class experience

Mobile networks and device conditions can change the way a page loads. Test the experience your audience uses.

Evidence before conclusions

Results should show what was measured, where it was measured, and what was unavailable. We avoid invented carrier or regional metrics.

What we measure

Available scan results may include Lighthouse-based page performance and network-response observations, depending on the configured scan path.

  • Website performance signals from actual scan output.
  • Endpoint response and latency where measurement is supported.
  • Transparent scope and methodology notes for interpreting results.
What we will not do

We will not present planned probe locations as active, invent performance scores, or turn a single test into a claim about every carrier or country.

Features without connected data are labelled as coming soon.
